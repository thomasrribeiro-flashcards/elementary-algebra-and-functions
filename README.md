# Elementary Algebra And Functions

Use variables, expressions, equations, inequalities, functions, graphs, and
elementary nonlinear models fluently and interpretively.

## Scope

- Subject: `mathematics`
- Learner level: foundational; designed for a novice who has completed the
  prerequisite arithmetic deck but has not studied algebra.
- Confirmed mathematical prerequisites: the complete
  `mathematics/number-sense-and-arithmetic` deck, including arithmetic
  expressions and operation order; signed, fractional, and decimal arithmetic;
  ratios and proportional tables; percent; units; estimation; and inverse-operation
  checks.
- Confirmed tools: none. Calculator, graphing software, and computer algebra are
  not assumed.
- What mastery enables: translating among situations, symbolic expressions,
  equations, inequalities, tables, and coordinate graphs; solving and checking
  elementary linear models; interpreting functions and linear systems; and using
  exponent, polynomial, factoring, and quadratic ideas as a bridge to precalculus.
- Deliberate exclusions: formal proof; geometry-specific theory; rational and
  radical function analysis; logarithms; trigonometry; complex numbers; and
  calculator- or programming-dependent methods. These belong to later decks.

Unconfirmed algebra knowledge is treated as unseen. The initial build stops after
the first chapter's novice-first pilot and cold-start audit.

## Chapter map

Only the first ordered chapter is authored during the pilot. Later filenames and
outcomes are curriculum plans, not scheduled cards.

| File | Topic | Learning outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| `01_variables_and_expressions.md` | Variables and expressions | Interpret variables, read expression structure, translate short verbal phrases, substitute values, and evaluate across symbolic, contextual, and tabular representations. |
| `02_equivalent_expressions.md` | Equivalent expressions | Use operation properties and the distributive property to rewrite, combine like terms, and check whether expressions agree for every allowed value. |
| `03_equations_and_equality.md` | Equations and equality | Interpret solutions, preserve equality, solve and check one-step equations, and diagnose common balance errors. |
| `04_multi_step_equations_and_formulas.md` | Multi-step equations and formulas | Solve linear equations with variables on one or both sides, rearrange simple formulas, and interpret special cases. |
| `05_inequalities_and_solution_sets.md` | Inequalities and solution sets | Solve, check, and graph one-variable inequalities while explaining reversal under multiplication or division by a negative number. |
| `06_coordinate_plane_and_relations.md` | Coordinate plane and relations | Plot and read ordered pairs, interpret axes and scale, and connect tables or equations with sets of plotted solutions. |
| `07_functions_across_representations.md` | Functions across representations | Decide whether a relation is a function and translate among verbal rules, tables, graphs, and function notation with meaningful domains. |
| `08_proportional_and_linear_functions.md` | Proportional and linear functions | Distinguish proportional from other linear relationships and interpret constant rate of change and initial value. |
| `09_equations_of_lines.md` | Equations of lines | Build, graph, and interpret linear equations from points, rates, tables, and contexts; compare common equation forms. |
| `10_systems_of_linear_equations.md` | Systems of linear equations | Interpret a shared solution and solve two-variable systems by graphing, substitution, or elimination with method selection and checks. |
| `11_exponents_and_polynomial_expressions.md` | Exponents and polynomial expressions | Apply exponent properties, interpret simple exponential change, and add, subtract, and multiply elementary polynomials. |
| `12_factoring_quadratics_and_nonlinear_models.md` | Factoring, quadratics, and nonlinear models | Factor elementary polynomials, solve checkable quadratic equations, connect factors and zeros, and contrast linear, quadratic, and simple exponential models. |

Chapter prerequisite edges and provided concepts live only in chapter
frontmatter. Inspect the generated human-readable graph with
`flashcards deck prerequisites .`; do not maintain a second prose graph here.
